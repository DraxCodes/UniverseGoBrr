UniverseGoBrr build status

Updated 2026-11-24 · branch wip/layer-3 · edited in the same commit as docs/issues/status.md

V1 is: fly from a galaxy arm down to a lit planet, cross the distance between systems with a real travel time, and send a friend the view. No gameplay. Layer 6 (walking on a surface) is not in V1.

Right now

Layer 4, travel. The frame rebase and the approach probe are finished, and the galaxy now keeps its identity when you move: a star next door shows you the same sky. The placement direction is decided — the address grid is the world, and the picture is a view of it — so every light you can see is somewhere you can go. Next task is placement itself: without it nothing has a distance, and travel time and the compass both need one.

What to send to Neon

  • Layer 3 visual pass. The one real handoff. Tone-mapping, colour grading and arm density at 1080p are taste calls a stronger visual model is better placed to make. We score the result here against the rubric — the score never leaves this workflow.
  • Layer 5 nebulae. Volumetric light transport and procedural noise kernels are squarely Neon's domain. Worth asking once Layer 4 lands.
  • Layer 4 tier masks — ask first. Warp streaks are WGSL, but they are effects plumbing rather than procedural noise. Your call whether that is worth a branch.

Every handoff is a branch plus a written return brief, and you approve each one. Gate scoring and seat configuration stay here.

Waiting on you

  • Travel speeds: ADR-0010 proposes impulse 0.01c, warp 1 ly/day, transwarp 100 ly/day, wormhole 10,000 ly/day. Confirm or move any row; the model does not depend on the numbers.
  • Panning: drag, or a modifier-click?
  • Name: is UniverseGoBrr the product name?

Placement is no longer on this list: the direction is decided, and the remaining numbers (how many cells are occupied, how dense a neighbourhood is) are ours to pick.
