Cairn build status

Updated 2026-11-25 · update 11 · branch main · edited in the same commit as docs/issues/status.md

The target, in one sentence: standing still — in a system, on an orbit, anywhere — the first three seconds should say this is enormous. Arriving in a system should show neighbouring galaxies the way Earth's sky shows Andromeda, and beyond those the sky is a continuum where some of the brightest dots are also galaxies. And anywhere you point at, you can get to — slow if you want the journey, a wormhole if you want to arrive now.

V1 is: fly from a galaxy arm down to a lit planet, cross the distance between systems with a real travel time, reach other galaxies, and send a friend the view. No gameplay. Multiple galaxies are in V1 (roadmap 4b); Layer 6 (walking on a surface) is not.

Neon's tier masks are back (2026-11-25): HDR warp streaks and a transwarp slipstream, plus an end-to-end probe, on agent/antigravity-tier-masks. Next is the review and the perception gate here — not in Neon's workflow.

Right now

Travel works end to end. A jump is one committed state change: the address becomes the destination, the clock advances by distance over speed, and the view lands on the destination's opening view. Back and Forward walk between the places you have been, not mouse moves. The address is clickable lines that zoom out to the galaxy for free, a compass lists the nearest drawn stars plus where you came from, and right-drag pans the view. Layer 3 is finished at the rubric's ship bar: the galaxy reads as a band of light, the core is graded rather than clipped, and the home star stays findable. The repo is now one line — main — with the handoff branches and review worktrees removed.

Many galaxies now have a skeleton. The address ladder has one authority (universe → galaxy → sector → system → body), a galaxy has a real size for the first time, and every galaxy has a position in the root frame — so a trip to another galaxy is a real journey now, priced like any other, instead of being refused for want of a layout. Any method prices any trip; a wormhole costs the same fixed hour whether it is next door or 6×10²² metres away. Next is the part you actually see: sectors in the address, then the field of neighbours — resolved galaxies, smudges, and ambiguous points. Nebulae are beauty and wait until after the base.
